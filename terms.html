Terms & Disclaimer

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Never eat a wild mushroom based on this app. Fungal Forecast cannot identify a mushroom, and some deadly species closely resemble popular edibles. Misidentification can cause serious illness, organ failure, or death.

1. What this app is

Fungal Forecast is a free hobby project. It combines recent rainfall, public sighting records, and map data to produce a rough, heuristic estimate of whether conditions might favor certain edible mushrooms in an area. It is not a scientific model, a field guide, or an identification service.

2. Not expert advice

Nothing in the app is professional, medical, or mycological advice. Species notes (habitat, season, rain thresholds, edibility comments) are general summaries and may be incomplete, simplified, or wrong for your region. Edibility and look-alikes vary by location, and some people react badly to species that are widely considered edible.

Before consuming any wild mushroom, confirm its identification yourself using current field guides, spore prints, and ideally an experienced forager or local mycological society. If you are not certain, do not eat it. If you or someone else becomes ill after eating a wild mushroom, contact poison control (in the US, 1-800-222-1222) or emergency services right away.
